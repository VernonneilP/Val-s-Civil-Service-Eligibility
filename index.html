Everything About PD 907 (Honor Graduate Eligibility)

What is Presidential Decree No. 907?

PD 907 grants Civil Service Eligibility to college graduates who completed their degrees with Latin Honors (Summa Cum Laude, Magna Cum Laude, Cum Laude) from recognized universities like National University MOA. This grants her the Career Service Professional Eligibility without having to sit for the 4-hour CSE board exam!

Photo Specifications (Very Strict!)

  • Passport size (4.5 cm x 3.5 cm)
  • White background with full face, neutral expression, and ears visible
  • Important: Handwritten name tag at the bottom showing: Last Name, First Name, Middle Initial with her signature signature OVER the printed name.

Where to File the Application?

She can file at:

  • CSC Regional Office / Field Office: CSC NCR (National Capital Region) or any CSC Regional Office near your location as a conduit filing.
  • Because NU MOA is under CSC NCR jurisdiction, submitting via CSC NCR or conduit office makes evaluation quick.
