✨ Step-by-Step Application Guide

1

Request Records from NU MOA

Secure an official Transcript of Records (TOR) and Latin Honor Certificate bearing the school seal. Confirm that the registrar has transmitted the official Master List of Honor Graduates to the CSC.

2

Prepare CS Form 101-HGE & Photos

Download and accomplish CS Form 101-HGE. Prepare 3 passport-sized photos on a white background with a handwritten nametag (Signature over Printed Name).

3

Submit to Civil Service Commission

Submit the complete application folder to the CSC Regional/Field Office (CSC NCR) or via conduit filing at your nearest CSC office.

4

Evaluation Fee Payment

Pay the non-refundable evaluation fee of ₱200.00 upon document submission.

5

Claim Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)

Once evaluated and approved, pay the ₱300.00 processing fee to officially claim your Certificate of Eligibility.

💖 Note for Val: Honor Graduate Eligibility grants full Career Service Professional Level privileges with no expiration date.
